# Governance, processors, and transfers

- Identify missing evidence for records of processing, controller-processor instructions, sub-processors, and international data flows.
- Do not infer that a vendor, cloud region, remote support, or API call constitutes a particular legal transfer arrangement without contractual and factual evidence.
- Escalate processor due diligence, Article 28 terms, transfer safeguards, and DPIA thresholds to the accountable controller, DPO, or counsel.

**SUPERVISORY GUIDANCE:** BfDI material illustrates records of processing, technical/organisational measures, and DPIA documentation. It is guidance, not a universal implementation template.

Sources: https://www.bfdi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Broschueren/INFO6.pdf and https://www.bfdi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Muster/Muster_Hinweise_DSFA.pdf
