# Tokens and components

- Review whether tokens express reusable decisions such as colour, typography, spacing, elevation, motion, and semantic roles rather than incidental values.
- Review component APIs for coherent variants, states, responsive behavior, composition boundaries, and naming.
- Check visible and non-visible states: default, hover, focus, disabled, selected, loading, error, empty, and validation states where applicable.
- Avoid extracting a token or component solely to remove a one-off value. Reuse requires a shared meaning or change reason.

**ESTABLISHED PRACTICE:** Tokens and components reduce inconsistency when they are documented, governed, and adopted; they do not guarantee a good interface by themselves.
