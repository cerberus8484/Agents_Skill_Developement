# Data lifecycle and rights

- Examine whether collection, fields, defaults, access, retention, deletion, exports, and visibility appear proportionate to the stated purpose. A technical observation does not decide necessity or proportionality.
- Identify evidence needed for transparency and rights workflows: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and safeguards around automated decisions.
- Treat backups, logs, analytics, test data, and derived datasets as lifecycle questions when personal data may be present.
- Hand off retention schedules, legal holds, identity verification, and rights-deadline decisions to the responsible privacy function.

**TECHNICAL RECOMMENDATION:** Make lifecycle states and deletion paths observable and testable where the architecture permits.
