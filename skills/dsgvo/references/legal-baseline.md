# Legal baseline

**LAW TEXT:** Regulation (EU) 2016/679 is the primary source. This skill uses it as a routing reference, not as a substitute for legal interpretation.

- Article 5: processing principles, including lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity/confidentiality, and accountability.
- Articles 6 and 9: lawful processing and special categories require fact-specific legal analysis.
- Articles 12–22: transparency and data-subject rights.
- Articles 24, 25, 28, 30, 32, 33–35, and 44 onward: controller accountability, design/default, processors, records, security, breaches, DPIA, and transfers.

Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/DE/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679
