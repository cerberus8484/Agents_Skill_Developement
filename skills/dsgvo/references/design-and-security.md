# Design, default settings, and security

- Review data minimisation, default visibility, purpose separation, access boundaries, and configuration choices when relevant artefacts are supplied.
- Treat encryption, access control, logging controls, resilience, and testing as potential technical and organisational measures; their appropriateness is risk- and context-dependent.
- Escalate suspected personal-data breaches, high-risk processing, or missing risk analysis. Do not decide notification duties or DPIA necessity.

**SUPERVISORY GUIDANCE:** EDPB Guidelines 4/2019 (final) explain Article 25 data protection by design and by default.
