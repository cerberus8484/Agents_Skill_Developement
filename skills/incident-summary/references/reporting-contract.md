# Reporting contract

## SOURCE FACT

NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 presents incident response as part of cybersecurity risk management, with detection, response, and recovery activities. It does not turn a report into a response authorization.

NIST SP 800-86 describes practical computer and network forensic guidance and explicitly says that it is not an all-inclusive step-by-step guide. A report must therefore state the limits of its supplied evidence rather than imply completeness.

## FRAMEWORK INTERPRETATION

`INTERIM` and `FINAL` describe the presentation of the supplied investigation packet. `FINAL` never means final incident status, complete evidence collection, analyst approval, ticket closure, or production authorization.

## OUR RECOMMENDATION

Summarize scope, established facts, copied assessment, uncertainty, and material open points without resolving them.
