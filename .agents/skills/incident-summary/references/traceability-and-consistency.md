# Traceability and consistency

## SOURCE FACT

NIST SP 800-92 provides high-level practical guidance for effective log-management processes; it is not a step-by-step implementation guide. Traceability of a specific summary is therefore a framework contract built on supplied identifiers, not a claim of universal log completeness.

## FRAMEWORK INTERPRETATION

Verify without repair:

- cited E### references exist in supplied evidence;
- cited F### references exist in supplied findings;
- cited H### references and their evidence links exist;
- a completed check is not simultaneously open;
- entities and timestamps are supplied;
- detection validity, activity assessment, and confidence are copied exactly; and
- selected summary mode matches supplied status.

For each material conflict, write `SUMMARY CONSISTENCY WARNING` with the exact missing or contradictory value. Do not invent an ID, fact, timestamp, relationship, or reconciliation.

## OUR RECOMMENDATION

When contradictory evidence prevents a coherent narrative, make the contradiction and its practical impact visible before the executive summary.
