# Roles, scope, and lawfulness

- Identify candidate personal-data elements and flows, but state ambiguity where identifiability, combination with other data, or actor access is unknown.
- Distinguish controller, joint controller, and processor as a fact-sensitive role question. Do not assign a role or contract conclusion from a code fragment alone.
- Record purpose, recipient, retention, location, and processor facts needed for a privacy review. Do not invent a lawful basis.
- Flag special-category data, children, profiling, and automated decisions as escalation triggers, not automatic findings.

**SUPERVISORY GUIDANCE:** EDPB guidance explains data protection by design/default and controller-processor responsibilities; authorities and courts apply law to the facts.
